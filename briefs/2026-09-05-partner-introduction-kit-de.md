# Okolo partner introduction kit — German

Prepared 5 September 2026. Drafts only; no messages sent. General organizer/service proposal, not an offer of audience reach or an assertion of an existing partnership.

## You do not need an existing network

Prior acquaintance is not a requirement. The restriction concerns the **channel and purpose** of the first contact, especially unsolicited promotional email, calls and electronic messages. Use one of these routes to create the first conversation:

1. Send a personalized **postal one-pager** to the organization, addressed to the responsible function such as `Leitung Veranstaltungsmanagement / Marketing`. WKO describes postal advertising as operating under an opt-out model, unlike promotional email. Use only necessary public business data and respect any objection.
2. Meet the organizer at a **public event, trade fair, hackathon, information session or business-network event**. Give the 20-second explanation, show the live showcase and ask whether you may email the details. Record the person's name, date and what they agreed to receive.
3. Respond through a channel that **explicitly invites supplier, partnership, innovation or pilot proposals**. Stay within the published invitation.
4. Use the **WKO Kooperationsbörse** for a genuine cooperation offer, such as a white-label/referral relationship with event agencies, web agencies or programme/print providers. Its terms exclude pure product advertising and ordinary customer acquisition, so state what both partners contribute and the shared customer outcome.
5. Send a genuinely **non-promotional technical/open-data question** to a public data support channel when that is the real purpose. Do not hide a sales pitch inside it. If the answer creates a relationship, ask separately whether the appropriate person wants the partner showcase.

Treat cold LinkedIn/Instagram/Facebook direct messages as electronic promotional outreach unless specific Austrian advice establishes otherwise. A public profile is not an invitation to receive a pitch.

For the first month, use a small named-account campaign: five personalized letters, two relevant public/networking events and five genuine agency/channel-partner approaches. The target is three needs conversations, not message volume. Each contact should name one actual programme and one reason a multi-location map might help it.

## Recommended sequence

1. Start with the existing Ars connection, or an in-person/invited conversation with an organizer. Obtain the recipient's agreement to receive a proposal and record when/from whom it came. Knowing a mutual contact alone is not consent.
2. Send the short personal email below, customized to one programme, with the live showcase link. An optional small inline screenshot should also link to the showcase; all essential information remains in the text.
3. Use the demo in a 15-minute needs conversation. Establish programme size, source, update work, distribution, owner, budget and deadline.
4. Send a one-page fixed-scope paid proposal after the need is established. State deliverables, source/brand permissions, fees/tax treatment, support/update limits, acceptance and payment milestones. Agree any follow-up timing rather than running an automated sales sequence.

For a truly cold commercial prospect, an addressed postal introduction or legitimate networking is a possible starting channel; respect data protection and objections. A generic contact form, social DM or an email asking permission for a pitch is not an automatic alternative to the electronic-advertising rules. An explicitly invited partner/product inquiry can be answered within its invitation's scope.

WKO says promotional electronic messages and calls normally require prior consent, interprets direct advertising broadly, and says published contact information is not consent. Its existing-customer exception has specific cumulative conditions; it is not a general B2B exemption. [Current WKO guidance](https://www.wko.at/information-consulting/werbung-marktkommunikation/werbung-per-telefon-fax-e-mail-sms-was-ist-erlaubt).

## Request to the existing friend

Use within the genuine existing personal conversation. Ask for an accepted introduction, not an unsolicited pitch forwarded to a list.

> Hey [Name], ich habe inzwischen eine vorzeigbare Demo für Okolos Festival-Karten. Damit können Gäste ein Programm nach Tagen und Orten erkunden, ohne eine App zu installieren.
>
> Könntest du bei Gelegenheit im Gespräch mit deiner Kontaktperson bei Ars fragen, ob sie Interesse an einer kurzen Vorstellung hat und ob ich ihr die Demo per E-Mail schicken darf? Am hilfreichsten wäre jemand, der das Programm oder die digitale Besucherinformation betreut.
>
> Mir geht es zunächst um ein kurzes Gespräch darüber, ob das für ein künftiges Programm nützlich wäre. Falls Interesse besteht, wäre eine kurze Vorstellung per Mail super.

Do not imply that this friend's request itself creates permission. Wait for the intended recipient's agreement.

Use “Wie besprochen” only if a discussion actually occurred. If the recipient accepted a third-party introduction without a conversation with George, use subject **“Programmkarte für [Veranstaltung] — Vorstellung über [Name]”**. Do not invent familiarity.

## One sentence to tailor to the recipient

- **Kinderkulturwoche:** “Gerade bei einem Programm an mehreren Kulturorten könnte eine gemeinsame Ansicht nach Tag, Ort und veröffentlichten Altersangaben Eltern die Auswahl erleichtern.” Age filtering is a proposed scoped capability, not a claim about the current generic demo.
- **Linzer City:** “Für eine Veranstaltungsreihe an mehreren Standorten könnte ein gemeinsamer Link Programm, Orte und Zeiten für Besucherinnen und Besucher zusammenführen.”
- **Ars:** “Nach dem diesjährigen Festival würde ich gerne erfahren, ob eine solche Kartenansicht für ein künftiges Programm einen konkreten Nutzen hätte.” Do not pitch a full new deployment days before the September 9 opening.

Replace a general sentence in the email with the tailored one; keep the message short. Data-only partners such as Linztermine need a separate technical/feed inquiry, not this managed-map sales proposal.

This is a commercial introduction, not a claim of a blanket legal exemption. Verify the recipient role, use minimal public professional data and respect objections.

> **Eine mobile Programmkarte für [Veranstaltung]**
>
> Guten Tag [Name],
>
> ich entwickle mit Okolo in Asten einen betreuten Kartenservice für Veranstaltungsprogramme. Gäste können Orte, Tage und Programmpunkte über einen einzigen Link oder QR-Code erkunden; der Veranstalter pflegt weiterhin seine vereinbarte offizielle Programmquelle.
>
> Für [konkretes Programm] könnte das die Orientierung zwischen den Veranstaltungsorten erleichtern. Eine fiktive Beispielkarte finden Sie unter **www.okolo.events/partners**.
>
> Wenn das für Sie interessant ist, freue ich mich über eine Nachricht an **hello@okolo.events**. In einem kurzen Gespräch können wir prüfen, ob ein kleiner kostenpflichtiger Pilot sinnvoll wäre.
>
> Freundliche Grüße
> Georgi Kostov · Okolo

Place one screenshot and an optional QR to the public showcase beneath the text. Include George's normal verified business sender details when actually preparing the letter. No letters, QR files, outbound emails, tracking pixels or recipient-specific analytics have been created or sent.
