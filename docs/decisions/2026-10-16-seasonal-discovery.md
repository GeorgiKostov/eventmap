# 2026-10-16 — Austrian holiday discovery

George requested Austria-wide Christmas market discovery, distinct festive icons and filters,
plus Halloween and other events in the upcoming holiday season. This explicitly authorizes this
seasonal Austria-wide slice; the four-weekend Linz validation gate remains unchanged.

The discovery themes are Christmas markets, other Christmas/Advent events (including Nikolaus
and Krampus), Halloween, and New Year. They are derived conservatively from event titles rather
than replacing source categories or introducing a new database field. The server applies the
same theme rules before viewport limits and aggregation. Existing family, price and setting
filters remain usable alongside a theme. Places are not seasonal events.

Selecting a theme opens the Austrian map and a Vienna-local date window from today through
January 31 of the upcoming/current winter. The visible controls explain the date and geography;
people can subsequently narrow the date range and map area. Map, list and detail icons share
the existing light-theme glyph system. English, German and Bulgarian labels are provided.

Coverage work uses approved official calendars and the repeatable crawl route. Existing records
are inventory, not evidence of a fresh source verification. Report audited sources, actual refresh
results and gaps separately; do not claim exhaustive coverage of every Austrian Christmas market.
Unknown dates/hours stay unknown, and intermittent markets must retain their actual occurrences.
New platforms still require the repository's authorization checks before automated collection.

Event database refreshes take effect independently of application deployment. A synchronized
checkout or Git push does not deploy the new interface; George retains the normal deployment step.
