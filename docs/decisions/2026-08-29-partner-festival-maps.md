# Partner festival maps

> Status: accepted for validation preview · Date: 2026-08-29

## Decision

Festival partners may receive a dedicated Okolo map URL backed by the same event facts, map,
filters and source links as the main product. The first concept route is `/aecfestival`.

The partner surface adds:

- a co-branded map header with a logo slot;
- a partner-only source filter, active by default;
- a date lens bounded to the festival programme;
- partner identity on matching event rows, previews and details;
- an official-programme link and privacy-safe view/filter/referral measurement.

The map pin grammar does not change. Category colour and event shape keep their existing meanings;
the partner-only filter is what makes the festival programme visually distinct. This avoids a new
logo pin that would compete with category, trust, precision and selection signals.

## Rights boundary

The preview uses an Okolo-owned placeholder treatment and plain factual naming. An organiser logo,
artwork, brand palette or claim of official partnership is not shipped until the organiser supplies
the asset and written permission covering the map, event pages and organizer-facing screenshots.
Preview routes remain `noindex` until that approval and launch scope are agreed.

## Product boundary

This is a validation-stage distribution showcase, not a general white-label platform. Partner
programmes remain code-configured and source-backed; no organizer accounts, self-service theming,
ticketing or schema changes are introduced.
