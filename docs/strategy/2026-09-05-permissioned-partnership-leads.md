This list contains only routes where an official website expressly invites partnership, cooperation, supplier, innovation, or membership enquiries. The invitation supports a first message **only within the stated subject**. It does not prove that the organisation buys digital services, and it is not permission to add the address to a mailing list or send unrelated follow-ups.

## Explicit paid-distribution route

The [OÖ Familienbund advertising page](https://ooe.familienbund.at/werbung/) expressly invites advertising and sponsorship enquiries at `events@ooe.familienbund.at`. This is a legitimate alternative to broad LinkedIn ads if you want to pay to reach Upper Austrian families. Ask for the current media kit, audience by channel, minimum spend, placement dates, and measurable link/QR reporting. Treat the Familienbund as a media seller here, not a prospective customer.

## One first-contact email

**Subject:** Kooperationsanfrage: mobile Programmkarte für [Festival 2027]

> Guten Tag Frau/Herr [Name],  
> Ihre Website lädt ausdrücklich zu Kooperationsideen ein. Deshalb möchte ich kurz prüfen, ob Sie für [Festival/Edition] auch digitale Besucher-Services als bezahlten Pilot einkaufen oder gemeinsam testen.  
>  
> Okolo verwandelt ein bestehendes Veranstaltungsprogramm in eine mobile Karte mit Tages-, Orts- und Programmfiltern. Sie erhalten eine stabile URL und QR-Grafik; wir übernehmen Einrichtung, Datenpflege und einen kurzen Nutzungsbericht.  
>  
> Beispiel: https://www.okolo.events/partners  
>  
> Wäre das grundsätzlich in Ihrem Kooperationsrahmen relevant? Wenn ja, sende ich gern eine einseitige, auf [Festival] zugeschnittene Skizze.  
>  
> Freundliche Grüße  
> Georgi Kostov

Keep the first email plain and short. Use one relevant link, no image attachments, and no generic deck. If they answer positively, send the one-page proposal with two or three screenshots tailored to their programme.

## Deliberate exclusions

Generic addresses for Kinderkulturwoche, Linz Tourismus, Linzer City, Familienkarte, PlusCity, RiS, and Linztermine remain valuable prospects, but their official pages do not expressly invite unsolicited commercial partnership proposals at those addresses. GEM2GO is included above only through its dedicated partner form. Use a warm introduction, an in-person opt-in, a specific data/support question, or an official procurement/intake route for the others.

Do not email several people at one organisation, add these contacts to a sequence, or send repeated unanswered follow-ups. Record the invitation URL and date beside each message so the basis and scope remain clear.
