## Not worth registering / dead ends
- Volkstheater (WP REST `veranstaltung` = 8 party entries), Haus der Musik (no structure), Kunsthalle Wien, Wiener Staatsoper, Musikverein, Filmmuseum: no usable structure or adult-only.
- Salzburg Museum: robots disallows `/wp-json/`. Rockhouse, Buehnen Graz / Next Liberty, Linz items and Klagenfurt/Villach/Wels municipal calendars are already registered.
- Domains that did not respond to curl (TLS/DNS), unchecked: khm.at, urania-wien.at, theatermuseum.at, schubert-theater.at, theater-asfalt.at, dasdachsbau.at, kuk-salzburg.at, land-sbg.gv.at.
- No new candidates found for St. Pölten, Wels, Villach (festspielhaus, VAZ, welios, landestheater.at: guessed paths 404 or no structure). Needs a second pass with manual URL discovery.

## Top 5 recommendations
1. **Technisches Museum Wien** (jsonld, one-line register; high family value).
2. **Theater der Jugend** (`tdj.at/kalender?monat=YYYY-MM`): biggest Wien kids-theatre gap; small HTML-card adapter.
3. **Lilarum** (Tribe REST, 118 events): kids puppet theatre; a reusable `tribe-rest` adapter will serve other WP Tribe sites too.
4. **MAK** (jsonld, 50 events/page) plus **Belvedere** (ItemList two-hop): museum family programmes.
5. **Graz Kulturserver digest parser**: replace the two `llm` rows with a deterministic parser of the single-item RSS digest.
Also cheap: Schoenbrunn microdata; NHM via typo3 adapter once the date format is checked.
