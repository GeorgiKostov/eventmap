# Okolo B2B sales package

Prepared 30 August 2026 for partnership development in Austria.

## Executive decision

Okolo is ready to sell one focused offer now: a **small managed pilot for a hosted, interactive programme map** for a festival, city programme, cultural campus, or multi-venue event. A second, separate conversation is appropriate for tourism organisations, municipal platforms, and media partners: **approved event-data exchange and distribution**.

The current product does not yet support a self-service organiser account, organiser dashboard, ticketing, or a proven national advertising product. Those should not appear in the sales pitch. The live account supports favourites and event/place submissions; it is a user feature, not an organiser console.

## Recommended offer

### Managed programme-map pilot

For one event, festival, cultural campus, or seasonal programme:

- a dedicated hosted URL;
- a branded interactive map with programme, venues, dates, filters, and highlights;
- structured onboarding from organiser-approved programme data;
- original short summaries and source linkback;
- German/English presentation where source material permits it;
- launch and update support during the pilot;
- aggregate usage and outbound-referral reporting with period and caveats stated;
- optional discovery through Okolo's public map where the event is relevant and approved.

The first commercial call should qualify programme size, number of venues, source format, update frequency, languages, launch date, ownership of supplied assets, and the decision-maker. Scope and price should follow that call; no unapproved price has been invented here.

### Data and distribution partnership

For tourism bodies, municipalities, event-calendar providers, and regional media:

- ingest only approved factual event data;
- preserve source attribution and direct linkback;
- align refresh cadence, deletion/correction handling, and geographic scope;
- agree the authorised interface or feed before automation;
- report discovery and referrals without claiming ticket sales.

This is not the same sales motion as the managed map and should use a separate message.

## Sales-readiness gaps to close before broad outreach

1. Add a named pilot or real reference before calling the demo a showcase.
2. Publish a one-page pilot scope: inputs, deliverables, timeline, responsibilities, reporting, and exclusions.
3. Create a sample results report using clearly labelled illustrative data until a real pilot exists.
4. Add a procurement-friendly company block: legal entity, billing location, data-processing posture, accessibility summary, and support contact.
5. Decide pricing only after George approves the commercial model.
6. Keep self-service organiser accounts, guaranteed reach, ticket attribution, and national rollout out of current claims.

## Outreach sequence

The workbook is a research and qualification list, not proof of permission to send unsolicited marketing.

1. Prioritise warm introductions, trade events, existing relationships, postal outreach, and inbound partnership forms.
2. For organisations with an explicit partnership or vendor channel, confirm the stated purpose still covers the proposed approach before using it.
3. Obtain or document consent before sending the sales email. A public generic or employee email alone is not consent.
4. Send one tailored message after permission, with two or three screenshots and links to the live offer and demo.
5. Follow up once with a concrete one-page concept for that organiser. Stop if there is no interest.

This is operational guidance, not legal advice. Have the final process checked against current Austrian TKG/ECG requirements before launch.

## Discovery-call checklist

- What visitor problem should the pilot solve?
- How many programme items, venues, and languages are in scope?
- What is the authoritative data source, and may Okolo process it?
- Who approves source text, images, map positions, and late changes?
- Which existing website, app, ticketing, and analytics systems must coexist?
- Which aggregate measures matter: map opens, programme interactions, or outbound referrals?
- What launch date, update window, and support response are required?
- Who owns the budget and signs the pilot?
